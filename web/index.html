Instruments

four independent modules

01 — FACE.ANON

Face Anonymizer

Detects faces, then segments the face itself rather than blurring a rectangle. A hand held in front stays perfectly sharp while the face behind it is destroyed.

BlazeFace + Segformer OPEN →
02 — DEPTH.EST

Depth Visualizer

Estimates relative depth from one flat photograph and back-projects it into geometry you can rotate — a coloured point cloud or a triangulated mesh.

Depth Anything V2 OPEN →
03 — POSE.TRACK

Rep Counter

Tracks 33 body landmarks and counts repetitions through a two-state machine. A rep only registers once the joint has passed through both full flexion and full extension.

MediaPipe Pose OPEN →
04 — SEMANTIC.IDX

Semantic Search

Searches by meaning rather than keywords. Drop in a pile of mixed files and it sorts them by what they are about, ignoring format entirely.

MiniLM + FAISS OPEN →
